ACCOUNT

Delete or disable your account

You can remove your TheLookCraft account and all of its data at any time — inside the app, or from this page without the app.

OPTION 1 · IN THE APP (INSTANT)

  1. Open TheLookCraft and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to Privacy & Data.
  3. Choose Delete Account & Data (permanent) or Disable Account (reversible — sign in again to reactivate).
  4. Confirm. Your profile, closet photos, looks, feedback and sessions are removed immediately and you are signed out.

OPTION 2 · SELF-SERVICE WITH AN EMAIL CODE

Loading…

OPTION 3 · EMAIL US

Send a request from the email address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Or write to support@thelookcraft.com directly.

WHAT IS DELETED

Account (name, email, password hash or Google identity), styling profile and personal photo, every wardrobe photo and its AI tags, saved looks and visualisations, wear history, feedback and all sessions. We keep nothing except anonymised server logs for up to 30 days for security. See the Privacy Policy.
