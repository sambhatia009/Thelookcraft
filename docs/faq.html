FAQ

Is TheLookCraft free?

Yes — getting started is free, and there are no ads. If we add premium features later they will be clearly optional.

Which phones does it support?

iPhone (iOS 16+) and Android (Android 8+). Store listings are coming soon; the website will link to them the day they go live.

How many clothes do I need to add?

Five pieces — a top, bottoms, shoes, a layer and an accessory — are enough for the stylist to start building real outfits. More pieces means more variety.

How should I photograph my clothes?

One piece at a time, laid flat or on a hanger, on a plain background with even light. The app compresses the photo on your phone and removes the background for you.

Does the stylist suggest things I don't own?

No. Every look is composed only from pieces in your closet. The optional “shop the gap” insight shows where one new piece would unlock many more looks — nothing more.

How does “Visualise on me” work?

You add one full-body photo in Profile. When you ask, AI renders a chosen look on that photo. The render is an approximation for deciding, not a product photo; you can save it with the look or discard it.

Who can see my photos?

Only you. Photos are stored in private storage and served solely to your signed-in account. Looks you share are shared by you, through your phone's share sheet.

Are my photos used to train AI?

No. Photos are sent to AI processors only to deliver the feature you requested (tagging, styling, rendering) and are not used to train our models.

Can I delete my data?

Yes — any piece, your personal photo, or your whole account, instantly, from Profile → Privacy & Data. You can also request deletion here without the app, or disable your account and come back later.

I didn't get the password-reset email.

Check Spam or Promotions — new senders often land there. The email comes from “TheLookCraft” with a 6-digit code valid for 15 minutes.
