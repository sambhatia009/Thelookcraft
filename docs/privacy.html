Privacy Policy

TheLookCraft ("we", "us") is a personal wardrobe and AI styling app operated at thelookcraft.com. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it.

WHAT WE COLLECT

  • Account: name, email address and a salted password hash — or your Google account identity if you sign in with Google.
  • Wardrobe photos: pictures of clothing you upload, the AI-generated tags (category, colour, style) and optional notes.
  • Profile & styling preferences: dress code, fit, colour palette, climate, optional gender and age range, and an optional personal photo used only to visualise outfits on you.
  • Activity in the app: saved looks, wear history, thumbs-up/down feedback on suggestions, and upload diagnostics (device platform, OS version, error messages) used to keep uploads reliable.
  • Location (optional): approximate location only while weather-aware styling is on. It is sent to a weather service and not stored.

HOW WE USE IT

  • To store your closet and build outfit suggestions and visualisations from it.
  • To process photos with AI services (clothing recognition, styling, image generation). Photos are sent to these processors solely to deliver the feature you asked for and are not used to train our models.
  • To send transactional emails you request (password reset, account confirmation codes). We send no marketing email.

SHARING

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the providers that run the service — cloud hosting, database, object storage, AI text/image processing, email delivery and weather data — each processing it on our behalf. Looks you choose to share are shared by you through your device's share sheet.

RETENTION & YOUR CHOICES

  • Data is kept while your account is active.
  • Disable your account to hide it and sign out everywhere; sign in again to reactivate.
  • Delete your account at any time from Profile → Privacy & Data in the app, or without the app at thelookcraft.com/delete-account. Deletion permanently removes your profile, photos, looks, feedback and sessions.
  • Delete individual photos, looks and your personal photo inside the app at any time.

SECURITY

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Photos live in private object storage and are served only to the signed-in owner.

CHILDREN

TheLookCraft is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

CHANGES & CONTACT

We may update this policy; material changes are announced in the app. Questions or requests: support@thelookcraft.com.
